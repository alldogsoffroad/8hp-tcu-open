## Standing bench facts (do not re-derive)

- Supervisor's echo (`0x22` read) = **mirror of our word | its bit 5** (bit 5 = its rail
  state). Stock engaged reads `AAB1` (rail up); ours `AA90` (rail down). Bit 0 never
  mirrors unless we send it — and when we send it, we get reset (all ages) or tolerance
  (+4–15 s, no grant).
- The supervisor powers up at **level `0x1F`** (probe reads it before we command). E14's
  no-disturb engagement: probe → send `0x841F` → HOLD in ~2 s. The old 1→7 ramp was a
  disturbance; it's still in the code for genuine cold starts.
- Two enforcement paths: ~5 ms hard reset on the bit-0 word; ~4 s trip on sustained
  ungranted setpoints (E7). Ammeter stays flat through both — protocol-level, not current.
- The stock engages ~+15 s after power-on (boot-era capture, §6.2i). Its commissioning is
  ~15 s end to end.
- Bench power-on ammeter: stock → 1.8 A within seconds; ours → 0.167 A directly (no
  spike). The rail is not an unconditional power-on default.
